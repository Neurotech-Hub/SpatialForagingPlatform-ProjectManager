# Meeting: ABC site tour — behavior-box fit and cleaning

- **Date:** 2026-08-31
- **Time / duration:**
- **Attendees:** NTH, ABC
- **Phase:** Alpha deployment / ABC install path (not Phase 5)

## Agenda

1. Tour of Animal Behavior Core facilities
2. Whether a retrofitted box can serve as the ABC behavior box
3. Cleaning protocol and what ABC needs for day-to-day flexibility

## Discussion

- Site tour at ABC to judge how SFM hardware sits in their space.
- Discussed **retrofitting an existing box** as the behavior box, and whether
  that fit is good enough for ABC’s cages, racks, and technician workflow.
- Cleaning protocol came up as a first-class constraint: ABC needs a path
  that survives routine clean and does not lock them into a one-off NTH
  assembly.
- For flexibility, ABC can **replicate the behavior-box assembly already
  running at Hengen Lab** rather than inventing a separate retrofit. That
  keeps the two labs on the same mechanical recipe (two-node setup) while
  ABC’s first pair is built.

## Decisions

- Prefer replicating the Hengen Lab behavior-box assembly for ABC over a
  one-off retrofit, so cleaning and maintenance stay the same across labs.
- ABC’s first deployment is a **two-node behavior box** (same class as the
  Aug 14 HLAB pair), not the n=9 field-test array.
